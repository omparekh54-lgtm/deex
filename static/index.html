THE PLAYBOOK

A strategy you can inspect.

Based on your PMS Rules Specification. Module D remains parked.

A

Fundamental eligibility

Non-financial companies. Market cap above ₹1,000 crore, sales ≥₹50 crore and positive PAT. Sales, operating profit, PAT and EPS growth thresholds are 15%, 20%, 25% and 20%.

Historical filing timestamps, pledge, cash flow and leverage guards control eligibility. Membership expires after 100 days.

B

Rally, then pullback

Start on the first close above 21 EMA. A valid rally gains at least 15%, at least 1% per day, within 30 sessions, with 1.5× baseline volume.

The first close below 21 EMA becomes the signal. Buy stop at its high, valid for the next three trading sessions.

C

Rank and allocate

Rank rally quality, pullback, fundamentals, relative strength, distance to highs and sector strength. Minimum score 60.

Reserve pending orders. Limit portfolio heat to 3.5%, positions to 10% of capital, and two holdings per sector.

E

Let the market set capacity

Trend, breadth, new highs and lows, India VIX and strategy feedback produce a smoothed regime score.

Capacity varies from zero to seven trades. The inherited policy resolves the draft benchmark name to Nifty MidSmallcap 400 and healthy capacity to six.

One shared trade lifecycle

Entry & risk

Fill at max(open, signal high). Recalculate sizing for gaps. Risk 0.5% per trade; stop is signal low × 0.995, capped to 5.5% below entry.

Exit & protect

Intraday stops fill at the stop or a worse gap open. Armed Leg 1 closes below yesterday’s 9 EMA: exit all below entry, or half above entry and protect the remainder at breakeven.

Finish & re-enter

Leg 2 exits at breakeven or on a close below yesterday’s 21 EMA. EMA decisions execute next open. Only the Leg 2 EMA exit can create a re-entry signal.

BEFORE THE NUMBERS

Good research starts with good data.

One full PMS strategy, with a reproducible historical stock universe.

Verify Angel One

  1. Use an active SmartAPI app key.
  2. Enter your client code, PIN and the current authenticator TOTP. Alternatively, use an existing JWT token.
  3. Set your backtest dates and starting capital. The 100-stock universe is automatic.
  4. Run the full PMS strategy and download the portfolio report.

Only login, the public instrument master and historical candle endpoints are used. There is no order-submission feature.

Broker verification may require approved app or IP settings. Credentials go to Angel One over HTTPS and are discarded after verification. The historical backtest uses the PMS dataset.

Run the full PMS strategy

Set PMS_DATABASE_URL in the Render environment to an existing, populated PMS PostgreSQL database. A dedicated read-only database role is recommended.

The database must contain the pms schema from your design_experience project: filings and normalized facts, prices, ownership/pledge, sectors, surveillance intervals, corporate actions, earnings announcements, historical index membership and official benchmark calendars.

Full runs read that dataset in one consistent snapshot. They recompute A, B, C and E using a fresh portfolio. They do not import stored PAPER selections or modify the database.

An API key alone cannot reconstruct historical fundamentals. Missing history blocks a full run rather than substituting present-day data.

How to read the report

Historical, not certified

All runs remain exploratory. The report identifies the selected 100 stocks, selection cutoff, dataset identity and any unresolved source assumptions.

Count complete cycles

A half sale is a fill, not another completed trade. Open positions remain marked in NAV. Undefined metrics display a dash.

Preserve your holdout

Development comparisons should end by 2024. Inspecting 2025 onward uses reserved data and does not certify an unseen out-of-sample test.
